Case Study

Healthy Little Me: A Cross-Border Healthcare Platform

A HIPAA aligned telehealth website built in Lebanon for a licensed pediatric dietitian, engineered to build clinical trust and convert patients across the United States.

17US States Licensed
100%Virtual Care
2Conversion Paths
4Focus Areas

The Challenge

Building a specialized telehealth platform for the highly regulated United States healthcare market from Lebanon means overcoming real technical, legal, and cultural barriers. Healthy Little Me, run by Nisrine Hamad, RDN, needed a HIPAA aligned, conversion focused platform that could build clinical credibility and convert US healthcare consumers from the first visit.

Design and User Experience

The interface balances clinical credibility with empathetic warmth for parents navigating complex pediatric health decisions. Core credentials, including Nationwide Children's Hospital training and an MS from Ohio State University, sit above the fold to reduce bounce rates, alongside a Health At Every Size aligned aesthetic and a mobile-first, touch-friendly layout built for parents searching on the go.

Dual-Path Conversion Design

Insured patients. Routed through the Fay Nutrition partner portal for instant Aetna, Cigna, UMR, and Oxford coverage verification.
Self-pay patients. Routed to direct calendar booking or a Venmo link, bypassing third-party insurance steps entirely.

Healthcare SEO Strategy

Because telehealth practitioners can only legally treat patients in states where they hold active licensure, the strategy targets state-specific search intent across 17 US states, including California, New York, Texas, Pennsylvania, Florida, Ohio, Colorado, Arizona, and Michigan, with long-tail clinical keywords such as pediatric ARFID telehealth and childhood tube feeding nutrition. Trust signals for Google's healthcare quality standards include verified provider credentials, institutional linkages, and embedded patient reviews.

Answer Engine Optimization

As search shifts toward AI answer engines like ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity, the platform is structured so those engines can parse, index, and recommend the practice accurately: direct question and answer content, clear entity association with recognized healthcare institutions, and machine-readable practice details such as hours, contact information, and delivery mode.

Built in Lebanon, Delivered for the USA

Colart's Lebanon-based team handled the design, conversion rate optimization, and performance engineering, while the platform itself was built for US deployment: healthcare scheduling integrations, compliant booking pathways, and a low-latency content delivery network for visitors across the United States.

Fianchetto Chess Center: Web Architecture & Sponsorship Engine

Architecting a scalable digital platform and sponsorship monetization engine for North Lebanon's premier chess hub, active in Tripoli since 2015 under the patronage of the Lebanese Chess Federation.

999+Students Trained
99+Tournaments & FIDE Events
4Sponsorship Tiers
$500+Top Monthly Tier

The Challenge

Fianchetto Chess Center needed a web presence that matched the prestige of hosting official Lebanese Chess Federation and FIDE rated classical tournaments. Corporate sponsors lacked a clear, professional presentation of tier benefits and packages, and major tournament news, including university partnerships with Jinan University, was scattered and unorganized, suppressing search visibility and audience engagement.

Colart's Strategic Execution

Colart built a custom, dark-themed responsive website with fast load speed, intuitive navigation, and direct WhatsApp lead generation for program enrollments and tournament sign-ups. A dedicated journal section was engineered to archive key achievements, including the North Lebanon Classical Chess Qualifiers, FIDE internationally rated classical tournaments, and Jinan University rapid competitions. Colart also stepped in as a headline Gold Sponsor alongside regional brands, actively supporting North Lebanon's sports and educational growth.

Sponsorship Matrix & Monetization Engine

A structured, interactive sponsorship system replaced ad hoc deals with standardized tiers, each with explicit marketing channels: TV screens, venue banners, flyer positioning, and player t-shirts.
