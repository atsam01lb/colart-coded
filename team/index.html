Founder

Samuel El Hindy

Samuel Hindy is the Founder of Colart Digital Marketing Agency, a creative entrepreneur and marketing professional focused on transforming ideas into distinctive brands, strategic campaigns, and meaningful digital experiences.

As a Meta Certified and Google Ads Certified professional, Samuel combines creativity with data-driven marketing expertise to develop strategies that help brands strengthen their positioning, reach the right audiences, and achieve measurable and sustainable growth.

With expertise in marketing strategy, brand development, digital advertising, social media marketing, content creation, campaign planning, website design, and web development, he founded Colart with a vision to bring creativity, strategy, technology, and performance together under one approach.

His experience extends across developing comprehensive marketing strategies, defining target audiences and brand positioning, planning and managing digital campaigns, building content strategies, optimizing paid advertising, and creating digital experiences that support both brand awareness and business objectives.

Driven by the belief that every brand has a unique story and potential, Samuel approaches each project with a strong focus on identity, innovation, strategy, and purpose. His goal is not simply to make brands visible, but to build strong identities, meaningful connections, and digital ecosystems designed for long-term growth.

Through Colart, Samuel continues to combine creative thinking with strategic marketing and digital technology, helping businesses turn their ideas into brands that connect, perform, and leave a lasting impression.
