Colart Shop FAQ

What is Colart Shop?

Colart Shop is Colart's branded uniforms line. It offers workwear like chef suits, aprons, polos, t-shirts, hoodies, medical robes and caps, made with your business logo and brand colors.

How do I get prices and order?

Tap Ask for Price on any item to message Colart on WhatsApp. The team will share prices, sizes and branding options, then send a mockup with your logo before production.

Can you add our logo to the uniforms?

Yes. Every item is made to carry your branding, including your logo, brand colors and, where needed, staff names.

We don't have a logo yet. Can Colart help?

Yes. Colart is a full-service branding agency, so the team can design your logo and brand identity first, then apply it to your uniforms.

Who are the uniforms for?

Restaurants, cafés, bakeries, hotels, clinics and medical centers, shops and companies that want their team to look consistent and on-brand.

Do you deliver outside Tripoli?

Colart works with businesses across Lebanon and the GCC. Contact the team on WhatsApp to confirm delivery to your location.
