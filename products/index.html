Colart Products FAQ

What is Menus by Colart?

Menus by Colart is a digital, QR-code menu platform for restaurants, cafes, and bakeries. Each menu is designed, hosted, and kept up to date by Colart at its own web address, so guests can scan and browse on any phone.

What is Price Lists by Colart?

Price Lists by Colart is a digital price list service for businesses like salons, grooming lounges, and other service or retail businesses, shared with customers through a link or a QR code instead of printed pricing.

What is Requests by Colart?

Requests by Colart is a digital song-request platform built for karaoke bars and entertainment venues. Guests submit a song request from a branded page on their phone, and it lands straight in the venue's WhatsApp.

Are these products built and hosted by Colart?

Yes. All three products are designed, built, and hosted by Colart, connected to the main Colart brand, and available as an option alongside Colart's marketing and design services.
