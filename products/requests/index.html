Requests by Colart FAQ

What is Requests by Colart?

Requests by Colart is a karaoke song-request platform, a dedicated hub where karaoke bars and entertainment venues keep their song requests running smoothly, all through one branded page.

How does the song request system work?

Guests open the venue's branded page on their phone, submit a song request, and it lands straight in the venue's WhatsApp, no app to download. The whole thing takes guests under 15 seconds.

Do guests need an app for song requests?

No. Guests use a mobile web page, no app download or account creation required on their end.

Who receives the song requests?

Requests go straight to the venue's WhatsApp, so staff or the DJ get them immediately without checking a separate dashboard.

Is the request page branded to my venue?

Yes. Every partner gets their own colors, logo, and hero photo, so the request page feels like a natural extension of the venue, never a generic template.

Can Colart create a custom digital request system for my business?

Yes. Colart designs, builds, and hosts a tailored request page for karaoke bars and entertainment venues. Reach out through the Contact page or WhatsApp to get started.
