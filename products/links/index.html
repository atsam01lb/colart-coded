Links by Colart FAQ

What is Links by Colart?

Links by Colart is a link-in-bio platform. Each business's page is designed, hosted, and kept current by Colart at its own web address, so visitors can tap one link in your Instagram bio to reach everything that matters, your website, WhatsApp, menu, social profiles, and more.

How does a link-in-bio page work?

A single link is placed in your Instagram, TikTok, or other social bio. Visitors tap it and it opens a branded page listing every link you want them to see.

Can I update my links after the page is live?

Yes. Links, buttons, and their order can all be updated on the page at any time, without ever changing the link in your Instagram bio itself.

Which businesses can get a link-in-bio page?

A link-in-bio page works well for restaurants, cafes, salons, service businesses, creators, and any business that wants to point bio visitors to more than one place.

Do visitors need an app to view the page?

No. Visitors open the full page straight from their phone's browser right after tapping the link, no app or download needed on their end.

How do I get a link-in-bio page made?

Reach out through the Contact page or WhatsApp with the links and branding you want included, and Colart will design, build, and host your link-in-bio page, then hand you a single link ready for your Instagram bio.
