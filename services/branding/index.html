Branding FAQ

What does Colart's branding service include?

Logo design, brand identity systems, and brand guidelines that give a business a distinct, consistent visual presence across every touchpoint.

How much does branding cost in Lebanon?

Branding is scoped to what your business needs, from a standalone logo to a full identity system. Reach out through our Contact page and we'll help figure out the right scope.

Do you rebrand existing businesses, or only build new brands?

Both. We work with new businesses building a brand from scratch and existing businesses that want a more consistent, professional identity.

Do you work with businesses in Beirut, Jbeil, or Batroun?

Yes. Colart provides branding for businesses across Lebanon, including Tripoli, Beirut, Jbeil, and Batroun, as well as the GCC and beyond.

Can branding be combined with graphic design and a website?

Yes. Branding is commonly paired with Graphic Design for ongoing collateral and Website Development so the new identity carries through to your site.
