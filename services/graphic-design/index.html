Graphic Design FAQ

What does Colart's graphic design service include?

Packaging design and general graphic design work, including print and marketing collateral and everyday brand visuals, for businesses that already have or are building a brand identity.

How is graphic design different from branding?

Branding builds the core identity, the logo, colors, and system. Graphic design is the ongoing visual work built on top of it, packaging, print materials, and day-to-day brand collateral.

Do you design packaging for products?

Yes. Packaging design is one of Colart's core graphic design services, built to make products look sharper and more market-ready.

Do you work with businesses in Beirut, Jbeil, or Batroun?

Yes. Colart provides graphic design for businesses across Lebanon, including Tripoli, Beirut, Jbeil, and Batroun, as well as the GCC and beyond.

How much does graphic design cost?

Pricing depends on the scope, whether it's a single packaging project or ongoing collateral. Reach out through our Contact page and we'll help scope it out.
