Website Development FAQ

What does Colart's website development service include?

Custom website design and build, landing and e-commerce pages, ongoing maintenance and updates, and speed/SEO optimization, all handled under one roof.

How much does a website cost in Lebanon?

Website pricing depends on the number of pages, features like e-commerce or booking, and the timeline. Reach out through our Contact page with a bit about your project and we'll put together a proposal.

Do you build websites for businesses in Beirut, Jbeil, or Batroun?

Yes. Colart builds and maintains websites for businesses across Lebanon, including Tripoli, Beirut, Jbeil, and Batroun, as well as the GCC and beyond. Most of the process happens online.

Can you add a digital menu or price list to my website?

Yes. For restaurants and cafés, we can connect your website to Menus by Colart for a QR-code digital menu, and for salons or service businesses, to Price Lists by Colart for a shareable digital price list.

Do you maintain the website after it's launched?

Yes, ongoing maintenance and updates are available so your site stays secure, current, and running smoothly after launch.
