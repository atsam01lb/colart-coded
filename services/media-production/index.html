Media Production FAQ

What does Colart's media production service include?

Photography, videography, and reels or short-form video production, built to capture a brand's story and support marketing across platforms.

Do you offer photography for restaurants and product businesses?

Yes. Media production covers product, space, team, and launch photography, commonly used by restaurants, cafés, and retail brands across Lebanon.

Do you work with businesses in Beirut, Jbeil, or Batroun?

Yes. Colart provides media production for businesses across Lebanon, including Tripoli, Beirut, Jbeil, and Batroun, as well as the GCC and beyond.

How much does photography or video production cost?

Pricing depends on the scope, location, and deliverables needed. Reach out through our Contact page and we'll help figure out the right scope for your project.

Can media production be combined with social media management?

Yes. Media production is commonly paired with Social Media Management, since the photos and videos we produce become the content used across your channels.
