Case Study

Atlantis Laundry Instagram Growth

A social media marketing and Meta Ads campaign that took a dry cleaning business in Doha, Qatar from zero followers to a real, engaged Instagram audience.

0Starting Followers
24.1KFollowers Gained
$500Monthly Budget
3Months

The Goal

Atlantis Laundry needed a real Instagram presence to build local brand awareness and generate direct inquiries for its dry cleaning and laundry services in Doha, Qatar. The account was starting from zero, with no existing audience to build on.

The Approach

Colart combined ongoing social media management with a Meta Ads campaign on Instagram and Facebook, targeting a Qatar based audience with a $500 monthly budget across paid media and content management.

The Result

In 3 months, the account grew from 0 to 24,100 real Instagram followers, giving Atlantis Laundry an owned audience it can keep reaching directly through future content and offers.

Nakha Khasa Instagram Growth

How one organic Instagram Reel turned local food culture into millions of views for a Lebanese bakery known for manakish.

3.0MViews
2.4MReach
70.6KInteractions
5.6KNew Followers

The Growth Objective

The goal was to make Nakha Khasa more discoverable beyond its existing follower base through Instagram content that feels native, local, and entertaining. Instead of paying for reach, the strategy focused on earning attention through the content itself.

The Organic Growth System

Attention. Open with a hook that creates immediate curiosity.
Relevance. Use local food culture, humor, and recognizable situations.
Retention. Keep the story moving so viewers have a reason to keep watching.
Engagement. Create content that invites reactions, comments, saves, and shares.
Discovery. Strong audience response helps Instagram recommend the content beyond the existing follower base.
Conversion. Turn new exposure into profile visits and followers.

The Flagship Result

The strongest example generated 3.0M views and reached 2.4M accounts, producing 70.6K interactions and 5.6K new followers, with a 21 second average watch time.

The Objective

Abou Hamze Restaurant's social media strategy is built around one goal: turn food-led content into sustained attention, local discovery, and real audience growth across Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok.

The strongest verified signal is the combination of 740.2K views and 312.2K viewers, roughly 2.37 views per viewer, showing repeat exposure rather than one-time consumption. That attention converted into 18.1K page visits and 2K new follows, an approximate 11% visit-to-follow conversion rate.

Social Media Management FAQ

What does Colart's social media management service include?

Content creation, content strategy, page management, copywriting, and monthly retainers, covering the day-to-day work of keeping a brand's social channels active, consistent, and on-brand.

How much does social media management cost in Lebanon?

Pricing depends on the platforms you're active on, how much content you need each month, and the scope of the retainer. Reach out through our Contact page and we'll walk you through a proposal that fits your business.

Do you manage social media for businesses outside Tripoli or Beirut?

Yes. Colart works with businesses across Lebanon, including Tripoli, Beirut, Jbeil, and Batroun, as well as the GCC and beyond. Most of the day-to-day process, content reviews, and planning calls happen online.

Can social media management be combined with other services?

Yes. It's commonly paired with Media Production for photography and video, Digital Advertising for paid growth, and with Menus by Colart for restaurants and cafés that also want a digital, QR-code menu.

How long does it take to see results from social media management?

It depends on the platform, the starting audience, and how much content is produced each month. Colart tracks performance monthly and adjusts strategy as the account grows rather than promising a fixed timeline.
